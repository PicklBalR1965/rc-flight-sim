How to drive

  • Left pad — slide left/right to steer
  • GAS to go · BRAKE to stop (hold it to back up) · Horn for fun
  • Only the Amphi Cruiser can drive into the pond, the pool and the creek. Hit the jump ramp in the big field!
  • Keyboard: A / D steer (left hand) · ↑ / ↓ gas and brake (right hand) · Space horn
